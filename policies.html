These guidelines explain how inquiries, session changes, delivery, and photo permission work at TOKI SATSUEI. We confirm the details of each booking with you before the session is finalised.

01 / BOOKING

An inquiry is not yet a reservation.

Sending a message or copying the inquiry form does not reserve a date. A session is considered confirmed only after you and TOKI SATSUEI agree in writing on the date, start time, session type, location, expected duration, deliverables, and final price through an official conversation.

Before confirming, please review the selected package and tell us about the concept, number of participants, and any schedule or access requirements that may affect the shoot.

02 / PRICES & PAYMENT

Agree on the full amount first.

The prices on the Sessions section are a guide and are subject to confirmation. Add-ons, additional participants, travel outside Quezon City, venue charges, and any other agreed costs should be discussed before the booking is confirmed.

Any deposit, payment deadline, payment method, refund arrangement, or other payment condition must be clearly stated and agreed in writing before you pay. This website does not publish payment account details. Do not send money to an account or contact that has not been confirmed through an official TOKI SATSUEI conversation.

Keep your payment confirmation or receipt. Ask us to clarify any amount you do not recognise before proceeding.

03 / CHANGES & DELAYS

Tell us as early as you can.

Please message us as soon as possible if you need to cancel, reschedule, change the location, or update the number of participants. New dates depend on team availability and any venue requirements.

Any deposit transfer, refund, cancellation charge, or rescheduling fee must be discussed and agreed before payment; no unlisted fee is automatically applied by this page. If you are running late, let us know so we can discuss the remaining schedule and what can reasonably be completed.

04 / PHOTO DELIVERY

Your package defines the deliverables.

Each package lists its included edited-photo count and session duration. Selected unedited JPEGs are also provided as stated in the package details. The number of edited photographs, agreed add-ons, and delivery format should be reconfirmed in the booking conversation.

Edited photographs and selected unedited JPEGs are delivered digitally through Google Drive within 7 days after the session. If a delivery link does not open or a file appears to be missing, message us through the same official conversation so we can check it.

Additional edits or requests outside the agreed package should be discussed first. We will confirm whether they are possible and whether an extra charge applies before doing that work.

05 / CONSENT & PRIVACY

Your photographs are not automatically public.

TOKI SATSUEI will ask for your permission before using recognisable client photographs in the public portfolio, archive, social posts, or promotional material. You may decline public use; that choice does not change your agreed session or delivery.

For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must be involved in arranging the session and must provide permission before identifiable photographs are shared publicly. Please tell us in advance if there are privacy, accessibility, or safety considerations we should respect.

The inquiry form on this website does not submit information to a server. It creates a message on your device and attempts to copy it to your clipboard so you can paste it into our Facebook conversation. When you send it through Facebook or Instagram, that platform's own privacy terms apply. Share only the details needed to plan the session; do not include passwords, identity documents, or payment credentials.

06 / LOCATIONS & SAFETY

Respect the place and the people in it.

Sessions are arranged around Quezon City. Transportation within Quezon City is included as stated in the package notes; travel outside QC is discussed before confirmation. The exact meeting point, access conditions, and any venue-related cost must be agreed in advance.

Venue photography rules, entry requirements, and permit requirements still apply. Please check them with the venue before the shoot. Outdoor plans may need to change because of weather, access restrictions, or safety concerns; we will discuss a practical alternative with you.

07 / QUESTIONS

Ask before you commit.

For anything unclear, message TOKI SATSUEI through the official Facebook or Instagram accounts. Please wait for written confirmation of your complete session details before travelling to a venue or making a payment.
