01 / BOOKING

An inquiry is not yet a reservation.

Submitting the website form sends a booking request for review; it does not reserve a date or hold availability. For a paid session, the booking becomes confirmed only after you and TOKI SATSUEI agree in writing on the date, exact start time, session type, location, duration, deliverables, and final price; the 20% reservation fee is received and verified; and we send an official written confirmation.

Free portfolio sessions do not require a reservation fee. They are confirmed only after the session details are agreed in writing and TOKI SATSUEI sends confirmation. Before confirming any session, please review the package and share your concept, number of participants, and any schedule or access requirements that may affect the shoot.

02 / PRICES & PAYMENT

Secure your paid session with a reservation fee.

For paid photoshoot packages, a reservation fee of 20% of the final agreed package price is required to secure the date and time. This amount is deducted from the package total; it is not an extra charge. The remaining balance is due on or before the photoshoot starts.

The 20% fee is calculated only after the package, add-ons, additional participants, travel outside Quezon City, venue charges, and any other agreed costs have been discussed and the final amount is confirmed in writing. Outdoor, event, and custom sessions are priced according to the scope agreed with you. Free portfolio sessions have no reservation fee or downpayment.

Payment instructions are provided privately through an official TOKI SATSUEI communication channel after the details and final price are agreed. The website booking form does not collect payments or publish account details. Do not send money to an account or contact that has not been confirmed through our official conversation.

Please keep your payment confirmation or receipt. A paid booking is not confirmed until the reservation fee has been received and verified and TOKI SATSUEI has sent written confirmation.

03 / CHANGES & DELAYS

Tell us as early as you can.

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel, reschedule, change the location, or update the number of participants. Rescheduling depends on team availability.

More than 48 hours before the session: You may choose a full refund of all payments made or one free reschedule, subject to availability.

Within 48 hours of the session, including cancellation on the day or at the scheduled start time: The reservation fee is retained as the cancellation charge. If you have already paid the full package amount, any amount above the reservation fee will be refunded. Please contact us as soon as you know you cannot attend.

No-show: If you have not arrived or contacted us within 15 minutes after the agreed start time, the session may be treated as a no-show and cancelled; the reservation fee will be retained. If you are running late, message us promptly. Late arrival may shorten the session so the next booking is not affected.

If TOKI SATSUEI cancels: You may choose a full refund of all payments made or reschedule without paying another reservation fee. No additional cancellation fee is imposed by this policy. Free portfolio sessions do not have a reservation fee, but repeated last-minute cancellations or no-shows may affect eligibility for future complimentary slots.

04 / PHOTO DELIVERY

Your package defines the deliverables.

Each package lists its included edited-photo count and session duration. Selected unedited JPEGs are also provided as stated in the package details. The number of edited photographs, agreed add-ons, and delivery format should be reconfirmed in the booking conversation.

Edited photographs and selected unedited JPEGs are delivered digitally through a private gallery link within 7 days after the session. If the delivery link does not open or a file appears to be missing, message us through the same official conversation so we can check it.

Additional edits or requests outside the agreed package should be discussed first. We will confirm whether they are possible and whether an extra charge applies before doing that work.

05 / CONSENT & PRIVACY

Your photographs are not automatically public.

TOKI SATSUEI will ask for your permission before using recognisable client photographs in the public portfolio, archive, social posts, or promotional material. You may decline public use; that choice does not change your agreed session or delivery.

For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must be involved in arranging the session and must provide permission before identifiable photographs are shared publicly. Please tell us in advance if there are privacy, accessibility, or safety considerations we should respect.

The booking form collects the details you submit: your name, contact channel and contact detail, selected session, preferred and alternative dates, preferred time, location, participant count, notes, privacy acknowledgement, and your acceptance of the booking and cancellation terms. The version of the booking terms you accepted and the time you accepted them are recorded with the private request so the team can identify which conditions were shown when you submitted it.

Your request is stored in a private Supabase booking table and is visible only to authorised TOKI SATSUEI administrators; it is not publicly readable. Supabase is the only record we use to track booking requests and their status. If the optional private Make.com webhook is enabled, the booking details may also be sent to the team's connected Gmail account as an administrative alert. This is an administrative notification only, not a booking confirmation.

Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect the form from automated abuse and may process technical signals under its privacy policy. Supabase, Make, Gmail, and Cloudflare process information under their respective service and privacy terms. Your request does not reserve a date, and no payment is collected through the form.

Share only the details needed to plan the session. Do not include passwords, identity documents, payment credentials, or other sensitive personal information. You can contact us through the official social pages instead of using the form.

06 / LOCATIONS & SAFETY

Respect the place and the people in it.

Sessions are arranged around Quezon City. Transportation within Quezon City is included as stated in the package notes; travel outside QC is discussed before confirmation. The exact meeting point, access conditions, and any venue-related cost must be agreed in advance.

Venue photography rules, entry requirements, and permit requirements still apply. Please check them with the venue before the shoot. Outdoor plans may need to change because of weather, access restrictions, or safety concerns; we will discuss a practical alternative with you.

07 / QUESTIONS

Ask before you commit.

For anything unclear, message TOKI SATSUEI through the official Facebook or Instagram accounts. Please wait for written confirmation of your complete session details before travelling to a venue or making a payment.
