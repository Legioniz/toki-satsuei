08 A LITTLE ABOUT US

We stay for
the feeling.

TOKI SATSUEI is a small photography team based in Quezon City. We are still learning, still building, and still finding our way of seeing. We care about the details that make a moment belong to someone: a gesture, a glance, a character becoming real for a little while.

Before a shoot, we listen for what matters to you. Then we work with a patient pace, available light, and attention to the small things. The goal is not to force a feeling into a frame, but to notice it when it appears.

09 A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the first frame.

A little clarity before you message us. If something is not covered here, ask and we'll confirm it with you.

We are a small team still building our public portfolio. We would rather be upfront about what is ready, what is included, and how your photographs may be used.

READ BOOKING & PHOTO POLICIES ↗
Do you have a cosplay or portrait gallery yet?

Not yet. The photographs currently shown are practice and detail studies, not a finished cosplay or portrait client portfolio. We are building the public archive gradually, and client photographs will appear only with the permission of the people featured.

What is included in each session?

Each package lists its session duration, number of edited photographs, and included details. Selected unedited JPEGs are included as stated in the package. Please confirm the exact deliverables, add-ons, and final price with us before your date is reserved.

When will I receive my photographs?

Edited photographs and selected unedited JPEGs are delivered through Google Drive within 7 days after the session. The exact package deliverables are confirmed in your booking conversation.

Where can we have the shoot?

We arrange locations with you, with Quezon City as our main area. Transportation within QC is included as stated in the session notes. Travel outside QC, venue access, and any venue-related costs are discussed before confirmation.

Can you post my photographs on your page?

Only with your permission. You can decline public use of your photographs, and that choice does not change your agreed session or delivery. For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must be involved and approve any public sharing.

Does the inquiry form book a session?

No. It prepares a message and attempts to copy it so you can paste it into our Facebook inbox. It does not send your details or reserve a date. Your session is confirmed only after the full details are agreed in writing.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Any deposit, payment deadline, or payment method will be discussed and confirmed with you in writing before payment. This website does not publish payment account details. Please do not pay an unconfirmed account or contact.
